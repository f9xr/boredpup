Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

The short version: BoredPuP has no accounts and stores almost nothing about you. What BoredPuP itself keeps lives in your own browser and never reaches us. But this site loads content from other companies - game developers, ad networks, and a few sites whose "featured on" badges appear on the home page - and those companies see your visit on their own terms. This page describes both halves honestly.

1. What BoredPuP stores

BoredPuP runs entirely from static files. There is no application server, no database, and no account system, so the only things BoredPuP writes are kept in your browser's localStorage and are never sent to us:

  • Favorites - the list of games you've saved with the ♡ heart button.
  • Recently played - the last games you opened, so we can show a "continue playing" row.
  • Catalog cache - a copy of the game catalog so pages load faster on repeat visits.

You can clear all of it at any time through your browser's "site data" or "local storage" settings. We cannot read it back, which also means we cannot recover it for you or delete it remotely.

2. Server logs

Our hosting provider keeps routine server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) for security and reliability. We do not run our own analytics trackers and we do not build personal profiles.

3. Games are third-party content

When you press play, the game runs on the developer's servers inside an embedded frame - not on ours. Those developers, and the ad networks they use, receive your IP address and browser details directly. We have no access to that data and cannot control it. If you would rather not be measured, an ad blocker and a content blocker such as uBlock Origin will prevent most of it.

4. Advertising

Free games here may show advertising. Two kinds appear:

  • Ads in the game frame, served by the game developer and their partners.
  • The walkthrough widget on a game page, which requests ads through GameMonetize. It is loaded from GameMonetize in a sandboxed frame with no referrer, and it cannot read anything on this page.

This site also publishes an ads.txt file declaring which ad systems are authorized to sell its inventory. Those networks are separate data controllers, covered by their own privacy policies rather than this one.

5. Other third parties that can see your visit

Even without pressing play, some pages contact other companies:

  • Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address on every page load. This is a deliberate trade for the site's typography, and it is the one part of the page you cannot block without the site looking wrong.
  • "Featured on" badges on the home page are images loaded from seven external sites (ShipThing, 1000 Tools, Smol SaaS, Smol Hunt, Product Hunt, Racoondr and Launchstag). Loading them reveals your IP address to each of them. We set referrerpolicy="no-referrer" on them so they are not told which page or which site the request came from, and they are lazy-loaded so they are not fetched until they are near the viewport. If you would rather they were not requested at all, a content blocker will stop them and the badges simply will not appear.

6. Children's privacy

BoredPuP is a general-audience site and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because we have no accounts, there is nothing to sign up for. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through a bug, contact us and we will help.

7. Do Not Track

An earlier version of this page said we respected the browser's "Do Not Track" signal. That was not true, so we have removed the claim rather than leave it there. Nothing in this site reads navigator.doNotTrack, and the site does not set a Global Privacy Control signal. In practice DNT is not honoured by most browsers, and it is being phased out of several of them, so it is a weak control in any case. The honest summary of this site is that the third parties listed in sections 3 and 5 do their own tracking, which is outside our control. We have reduced what we share ourselves: no analytics, no cross-site cookies, and no referrer on external images or frames.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we'll update this page and change the date above. Material changes are announced on the site.
