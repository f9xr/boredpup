About BoredPuP

Thousands of free browser games, one bored pup at the wheel.

BoredPuP is a place to scratch the itch when there's nothing to do. We collect and organize the best free HTML5 games - puzzles, racers, arcade classics, clickers, and everything in between - and put them behind a single "play" button.

Why we exist

Somewhere between "install an app" and "sit in a queue for a lobby" there used to be no middle ground. Games should open in a second, in any browser, on any device, and cost nothing. That's the entire idea: pick a game, click play, and be entertained.

How it works

Every game on BoredPuP runs entirely in your browser as HTML5 - no downloads, no registration, no paywalls. Our catalog is driven by game networks like GameMonetize, GameDistribution, and yupi.io, which curate titles from developers around the world. We index those catalogs fresh from their feeds, add small details like descriptions, tags, and instructions, and present everything in a fast, embarrassingly simple to use layout.

What we're careful about

  • Kids and parents - we aim for games that are wholesome and safe, and we do not collect personal information on this site.
  • We play no favorites: our "Good picks" and random button don't sell placement.
  • Our privacy policy is written in plain language, because it should be.

Who makes the games

The developers do - independently, all over the world, through the GameMonetize, GameDistribution, and yupi.io networks. BoredPuP is the friendly front door; the credits belong to the studios and hobbyists who build the games you play.

Found a broken game, or want something added? Tell us - we read every message.
